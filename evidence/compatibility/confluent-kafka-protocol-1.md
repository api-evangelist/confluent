 FULLY MANAGED | CONFLUENT CLOUD
 Harness Real-Time Data, Lower Your Costs – Any Scale, Any Cloud
 Confluent Cloud is the fully managed deployment of our data streaming platform. Its serverless Apache Kafka® engine powers the most efficient way to deploy and scale real-time data streams in the cloud. But streaming your data is just the start.
 See how Confluent Cloud equips you to maximize Kafka cost savings, streamline security, and integrate hybrid deployments.

 Cut Infrastructure Costs in Half With Autoscaling Clusters
 Say goodbye to over-provisioned and underutilized Kafka clusters. Powered by Kora, our cloud-native Kafka engine , Confluent Cloud automatically scales to ensure your infrastructure is always right-sized for your workload. You no longer have to pay for capacity you don’t need, cutting your infrastructure spend in half.

 Basic and Standard Clusters

 Enterprise Clusters
 Choose Enterprise clusters to deploy mission-critical use cases and protect sensitive data with private networking, autoscaling at GBps+ scale, and 32%+ throughput savings.

 Freight Clusters
 Take advantage of Freight clusters to see up to 90% throughput savings for high-volume workloads like logging, observability and AI/ML data ingestion.

 Multi-Region & Multicloud Clusters
 Replicate, sync, and share data directly from one Confluent Cloud cluster to another with Cluster Linking—even if the clusters live in different regions, public clouds, or organizations.

 Confluent Cloud for Government: Now FedRAMP Moderate Authorized
 Stop managing infrastructure and start delivering outcomes with the industry’s only fully managed, FedRAMP-compliant data streaming service.

 Offload Apache Kafka® Ops for 60% Lower TCO and Faster Dev
 Break past limits on your scalability, budget, and time and offload all your operations to Confluent Cloud with resilient, serverless data streaming available on all three major public clouds. With pay-as-you-go consumption and cost-efficient clusters for any workload, Confluent Cloud reduces the total cost of ownership (TCO) for self-managed Kafka by up to 60%—giving your team the freedom and focus to deliver your best work.
 Confluent Cloud’s flexible cluster options further maximizes the cost savings. Mix and match cluster types based on your use case’s unique requirements, all while ensuring availability of mission-critical workloads with our 99.99% uptime SLA.

 Complete Capabilities for Enterprise Security & Modern Data Architecture
 With the right combination of capabilities, a complete data streaming platform helps your entire organization migrate away from fragile, point-to-point integrations to gain secure, reliable, and real-time access to high quality data.

 Ensure Regulatory Compliance With Robust Security Controls
 Easily manage authentication , control access to cloud resources, encrypt data streams, and monitor & assess security risks . All security features—including RBAC, self-managed encryption keys, client-side field-level encryption, and audit logs—are built to meet your needs for data privacy regulations.
 Learn more 
 Easily Integrate Data Systems With Fully Managed Connectors
 Don’t spend months designing, building, testing, and maintaining Kafka connectors—and even more time self-managing them. Easily integrate Confluent with sources and sinks like databases, data lakes, and data warehouses using 120+ pre-built and 80+ fully managed Kafka connectors.
 Explore Connectors 
 Govern and Process at the Source for Downstream Data Reuse
 With Confluent Cloud for Apache Flink® and Stream Governance , you can shift processing and governance left to prevent bad data and lower compute costs. Build analytics and AI-ready data products to share, discover, and reuse across teams and use cases.
 Learn more about Shift Left 
 Use Tableflow to Turn Data Streams Into Analytics Tables
 In just a few clicks, Tableflow allows you to materialize Kafka topics and schemas as Apache Iceberg™ and Delta Lake tables. Feed the data lakes, data warehouses, and analytics engines that power your AI use cases with fresh, trustworthy, and enriched data, integrated with our partners’ catalogs.
 Explore Tableflow 
 Migration to Cloud-Native Kafka Has Never Been Easier
 Kafka Copy Paste (KCP), a free open source tool, automates migration to Confluent Cloud, cutting down migrations times from months to days. KCP eliminates a significant amount of the manual work involved, including cluster discovery and cost assessment, infrastructure provisioning, and data migration, helping you achieve a stress-free, near zero-downtime migration.
 Read blog Download the Migration Guide 
 Unify Your Data Across Global, Hybrid Environments
 Use the data streaming platform wherever your data lives—fully managed on 100+ regions across Amazon Web Services , Microsoft Azure , and Google Cloud , managed within your own cloud account via WarpStream , or self-managed in on-premises and private cloud environments with Confluent Platform .
 And with Cluster Linking , you can integrate data across hybrid and multicloud deployments, over public and private networks, to provide a single global data plane where all your environments are connected. This enables unified monitoring and management and eliminates the need for complex point-to-point connections, allowing you to easily:
 Link Confluent Platform and Confluent Cloud clusters
 Migrate data across Confluent Platform and Confluent Cloud clusters
 Share data across clusters, regions, and clouds on Confluent Cloud

 Confluent Cloud FAQs
 What is Confluent Cloud?
 Confluent Cloud is the industry's only fully managed data streaming platform. With Confluent Cloud, you can future-proof your data architecture and unlock data access, massive scalability, high data durability and availability, and stream processing and analytics use cases.
 You can get started directly in the cloud console or take advantage of integrated billing with your cloud service provider by signing up in the AWS , Google Cloud Google Cloud or Microsoft Azure marketplace. After you sign up, use the Quick Start Guide to get familiar with using the web UI or Confluent CLI to manage clusters and topics.
 How is Confluent Cloud different from open source Kafka?
 Apache Kafka® is a distributed streaming engine that powers high-throughput, fault tolerant, and highly scalable real-time data pipelines, stream processing, and event-driven data integration. In contrast, Confluent is a complete data streaming platform that goes beyond Kafka to offer enterprise-grade capabilities for securing, connecting, governing, and processing data streams with cloud-native autoscaling, enterprise-grade security, and pre-built and fully managed integrations.
 As the fully managed deployment of our platform, Confluent Cloud is powered by a cloud-native Kafka service, fully rebuilt for seamless scalability and hybrid cloud integration. See a complete comparison of Confluent vs. Apache Kafka or learn more about Kora, our cloud-native Kafka engine .
 What features does Confluent Cloud include?
 Confluent Cloud includes:
 Fully managed Kafka clusters (Basic, Standard, Enterprise, Freight) that offer elastic scalability, infinite storage, guaranteed resiliency (with 99/99% uptime SLA for multi-AZ clusters, low latency, as well as Dedicated clusters for workloads that require pre-provisioned resources
 Data replication capabilities for data migration and disaster recovery with Confluent Replicator and Cluster Linking 
 Enterprise-grade security features , including role based access control, encryption for data at rest and data in transit, audit logs, and private networking
 Metrics API for monitoring cluster performance, consumer lag, and other metrics
 Support for a wide range of client languages, including Java, Python, .NET, Go, Node.js , and CC++
 120+ pre-built Kafka connectors and 90+ fully managed connectors, available on Confluent Hub 
 Stream Governance , an advanced, fully managed suite of governance tooling, which includes Stream Lineage, Stream
 Confluent Cloud for Apache Flink® , a serverless stream processing service that allows you to enrich, join, and filter data and integrate AI/ML models with AI Model Inference
 How does Confluent Cloud pricing work?
 Confluent Cloud’s pricing is determined by the features used (i.e., stream, connect, govern, and process capabilities), the selected tier, and the specific task & its rate per processing unit, throughput, or environment. Monthly bills are determined by consumption of resources within your cloud organization.
 With Confluent Cloud, you can make annual commitments to minimum spend. With commitments, discounts scale with your usage across the entire Confluent Cloud stick,including clusters, connectors, Stream Governance, stream processing, and technical support.
